↩️ Returns & Refunds

Return Policy

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be in original condition with tags attached.

How to Return

  1. Go to Your Orders
  2. Select the item you want to return
  3. Choose a return reason
  4. Print the return label
  5. Pack and drop off at any shipping location

Refund Timeline

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we receive the returned item.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I track my order?

A: Go to Your Orders and click on the order to see tracking info.

Q: Can I cancel my order?

A: Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement. Go to Your Orders and click Cancel.

Q: How do I change my address?

A: Go to Your Account → Addresses to add or edit.

Q: Do you ship internationally?

A: Currently we ship to select countries. Check at checkout for availability.

Q: How do I contact support?

A: Visit our Contact page or chat with our AI Assistant.
